Five members of the Block & Bull community win 10 USDT every month, paid straight to their exchange account. Entry is free and takes about two minutes.

How to enter

  1. Open a free account through our Binance or Bybit link.
  2. Copy your UID: the account number in your exchange profile.
  3. Send it to our bot. That's your entry.
Enter on Telegram

No deposit, no trade, no payment. One entry per person.

Prize. 10 USDT to each of 5 winners, every calendar month, sent to the winner's verified UID within 7 days of the draw.
Who can enter. Anyone 18 or over whose Binance or Bybit account was opened through our referral link. We can only check accounts opened through our link, so older accounts don't qualify.
Entry window. The calendar month in India time. Entries close at 23:59 IST on the last day of the month. One entry per person and per UID.
How winners are picked. We verify every UID against our referral dashboard. Then we take the hash of the first Bitcoin block mined after 00:00 UTC on the 1st of the next month and use it to shuffle the verified list, ordered by entry time. The first five win. Anyone can recheck the result from the public block hash.
Announcement. Winners are posted in @blocknbulldaily by Telegram handle, with UIDs partly hidden, and messaged by @blocknbullbot.
Scam warning. We never ask winners for a password, seed phrase, API key, fee or deposit. Anyone who does is not us. Report and block them.

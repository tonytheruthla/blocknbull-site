Straight answers
Is any of this financial advice?

No. Block & Bull is an educational publisher. We explain what happened in markets and how the mechanisms work. We do not tell you what to buy, when to buy it, or what anything will be worth. Trading is high-risk and you can lose everything you put in. Talk to a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

Do you give signals or calls?

Never, and we will not start. No entries, no targets, no stop losses, no "next 10x." If a number appears on one of our cards it is something that already happened, with a source attached.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The daily block is free to read on Telegram and free to follow on social. There is no paid tier, no signals group and no course.

How do you make money?

Block & Bull earns through affiliate partnerships elsewhere on this site, and every one of those links is labelled as such where it appears. None of that changes what goes on a card. We have published things that are inconvenient for us, and we will keep doing that.

Who is it for?

People who want to understand markets rather than be told what to do with them. If you are brand new, the daily block is a way to learn the vocabulary in small pieces. If you already trade, it is a sourced read you can check in two minutes.

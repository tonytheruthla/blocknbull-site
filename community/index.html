Straight answers
Is the group actually free?

Yes, and there is no paid tier sitting above it. The exchanges pay us a commission on member trading fees and that pays for the room. You are never billed by us.

Do I have to deposit money to get in?

You need a verified account opened under our link. How much you fund it with, and whether you trade at all, stays your decision.

What is a UID and is it safe to send?

It is the public identifier on your exchange account, the same number support asks for. It cannot be used to log in, move funds, or place a trade. A password, an API key or a seed phrase can, which is why the bot never asks for any of them.

I already have an exchange account. Can I still join?

Tell the bot and we will check. Bybit lets you open a fresh account under a referral, while an existing account usually cannot be moved across. If the answer is no in your case, we will say so rather than string you along.

Do you give signals?

No, and we never have. You get the analysis and the level that invalidates it. The decision is yours and so is the outcome.

Is this available in India?

Bybit is. WEEX is not, blocked under FIU-IND direction in September 2026. Indian members trade on Bybit.

How long does approval take?

Usually the same day. We check UIDs by hand against the affiliate dashboard, so it is not instant, and a weekend request may wait until Monday.

Straight answers.

Are the welcome rewards real money I get immediately?

No. They are the exchanges' own promotional programs, typically released in tiers as you deposit and trade, with expiry dates and volume conditions. The headline figure is the maximum available under the program, not a sign-up gift. Read the terms on the exchange's site.

Is this financial advice?

No. Block & Bull is an educational publisher. We compare exchange terms and explain how things work. We don't tell you what to buy or when. Trading is high-risk and you can lose everything you put in.

How do you make money?

Through referral links. If you sign up through one, the exchange may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. We say so every time a link appears, and we only link exchanges that publish proof-of-reserves.

Which countries can use these exchanges?

Each exchange maintains its own restricted-country list, and it changes. Check availability for your country on the exchange's site before you register. Some jurisdictions, including the US, UK, Canada and Japan, restrict or block one or both of these platforms.

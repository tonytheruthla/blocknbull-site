Straight answers
Is the welcome reward cash I get on sign-up?

No. It's the exchange's promotional program, released in tiers as you deposit and trade, with an expiry date. The headline figure is the program maximum. Read the tier table on the exchange's terms page.

Is this financial advice?

No. Block & Bull is an educational publisher. We compare exchange terms and explain how markets work. We don't tell you what to buy or when. Trading is high-risk and you can lose everything you put in.

How does Block & Bull make money?

Referral links. If you sign up through one, the exchange may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. We label every link, and we only link exchanges that publish proof-of-reserves.

Can I use these exchanges in my country?

Each exchange keeps its own restricted-country list, and it changes. Check eligibility on the exchange's site before you register. The US, UK, Canada and Japan restrict or block one or both.

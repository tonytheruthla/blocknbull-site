Because that is what pays for it. The exchange pays us a referral commission on member trading fees and we write the guide out of that, which is why there is no charge and no paid tier. You get a 20% fee rebate set on your account at the same time, so the step is not one-way.

Do you give signals?

No, and we never have. You get the framework and the reasoning. The call is yours and so is the outcome.

Will you build my portfolio for me?

No. We teach how sizing and balance work and you apply them yourself. We are not portfolio managers and we never take custody of anyone's funds.

Who is it for?

Someone who has opened an account, or is about to, and wants to understand the process rather than follow calls.

What is a UID and is it safe to send?

It is the public identifier on your exchange account, the same number support asks for. It cannot be used to log in, move funds or place a trade. A password, an API key or a seed phrase can, which is why we never ask for any of them.
